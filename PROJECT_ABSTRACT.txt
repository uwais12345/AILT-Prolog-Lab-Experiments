                                PROJECT ABSTRACT

TITLE:
Personalized Product Recommendation and Intelligent Store Placement System Using Prolog

DOMAIN:
Artificial Intelligence | Knowledge Representation & Reasoning | Symbolic AI | Expert Systems

TECHNOLOGY STACK:
SWI-Prolog (100% Pure Logic Programming; Zero External Libraries; No ML; No Databases)

ABSTRACT:
Modern commercial recommendation engines predominantly rely on statistical machine 
learning and deep neural architectures. While effective at scale, these approaches 
suffer from significant drawbacks, including a "black-box" nature, excessive 
computational overhead, and a complete absence of transparent, verifiable reasoning. 

This project presents a lightweight, highly interpretable, and rule-based expert 
system developed entirely in SWI-Prolog. The system demonstrates how classical 
Symbolic Artificial Intelligence can solve complex retail recommendation and 
merchandising problems through formal logic. 

Customer transactions, product taxonomies, complementary relationships, and 
co-purchase frequencies are represented as declarative first-order facts in a 
structured knowledge base. Using Horn clause rules, the Prolog inference engine 
executes logical deduction, term unification, and chronological backtracking to:
1. Infer implicit user preferences and recommend category-specific unpurchased products.
2. Recommend immediate complementary accessories upon item acquisition.
3. Identify frequently co-purchased bundles.
4. Derive intelligent physical retail shelf-placement suggestions based on market-basket 
   affinity rules.
5. Provide native Explainable AI (XAI) capabilities by exposing the exact derivation 
   trees and logical premises behind every generated recommendation.

The application features a professional, interactive terminal-based frontend built 
directly inside SWI-Prolog, featuring structured menus, ANSI-formatted visual banners, 
and input sanitization. The system also exposes direct query interfaces for academic 
inspection, demonstrating fundamental AI concepts including SLD resolution, 
depth-first search, and closed-world negation-as-failure. 

Experimental evaluation across multiple test suites confirms that the system operates 
with deterministic accuracy, sub-millisecond query latency, and absolute interpretability, 
establishing it as an ideal benchmark and pedagogical model for symbolic knowledge-based 
reasoning in retail applications.
